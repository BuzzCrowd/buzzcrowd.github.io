Delete your Buzz account

This page explains how to delete your account in Buzz, the app by BuzzCrowd, and what happens to your information when you do.

In the app

  1. Open Buzz and log in.
  2. Tap Profile at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and everything linked to it is deleted straight away.

Without the app

If you can't open the app, email buzzcrowdapp@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my account. We'll delete your account within 30 days and let you know when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: username, email address, profile photo and login
  • Your videos and their preview images
  • Your check-ins, vibe ratings and comments
  • Posts you've reported and people you've blocked
  • Your notification token

We don't keep any of this after your account is deleted. Leftover video and photo files are cleared from storage within a day.

Questions

Email buzzcrowdapp@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
