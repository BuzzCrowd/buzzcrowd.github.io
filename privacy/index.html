Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information the Buzz app collects, why, who it's shared with, and your rights under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Buzz ("we", "us") is the responsible party. You can reach our Information Officer at buzzcrowdapp@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, username and password (stored securely as a scrambled hash; we can't see it).
  • Profile photo: if you add one.
  • What you post: videos and their preview images, check-ins, vibe ratings and comments, each linked to the venue and the time.
  • Location: when you check in, post or rate, the app checks on your phone that you're within 300 metres of the venue. Your approximate location is also used to show nearby venues on the map and in venue search. We don't keep a record of your location on our servers; we only store which venue you checked in at or posted from.
  • Notification token: a code from your phone that lets us send you notifications.
  • Reports and blocks: posts you've reported and people you've blocked.

We don't use advertising trackers, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell your information.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and show your posts to other users
  • To work out how busy venues are (check-ins, recent videos and ratings from the last 2 hours)
  • To send notifications you've allowed, such as when a venue you were recently at gets busy, and weekend reminders
  • To keep Buzz safe: reviewing reports, removing content that breaks our Terms of Use, and preventing abuse
  • To fix problems and improve the app

What other people can see

Your username, profile photo, videos, ratings and comments are visible to other Buzz users. Your email address and check-in history are not shown to other users.

Who we share it with

We use a few trusted service providers to run Buzz. They only use your information to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: hosts our database, accounts and media. Its servers are in London, United Kingdom, so your information is stored outside South Africa, with protections that meet POPIA's requirements.
  • Google Maps and Places: shows the map and powers venue search. Google receives your search text and approximate location.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) and Apple Push Notification service: deliver notifications.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect people's safety.

How long we keep it

  • Videos and their preview images are deleted 24 hours after they're posted.
  • Check-ins, ratings and comments are kept while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account (Profile → Delete account), we delete your profile, posts, check-ins, ratings, reports and blocks, and your login. Any leftover files are removed within a day.

Your rights

Under POPIA you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it. Email buzzcrowdapp@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days. You can delete your account yourself at any time in the app.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Regulator: inforeg.org.za.

Security

Your information is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so people can only change their own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Age limit

Buzz is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe a minor is using Buzz, contact us and we'll remove the account.

Changes

We may update this policy. If the changes are significant we'll let you know in the app.
